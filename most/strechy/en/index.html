Roofs

A small texture with a large effect on the whole street.

A visual notebook of the MOST project · September 2026

From a crop to a library

Library v2 contains four roofing families, each with three generated variants: varied wear, repairs and heavy damage. They are based on traceable historical photograph crops. The precise material of the grey plates remains unidentified.

Scale and repetition

The target module is 4 × 4 m at 2048 × 2048 px. This is a working target, not a historical measurement. All 12 designs are recorded as awaiting scale and seam correction. The 3 × 3 view helps expose repeating stains and discontinuities; it does not certify seamlessness.

The roof must follow the footprint

The geometry pilot uses a polygon straight skeleton to form ridges and valleys. The no. 1432 example includes a concave footprint. Slope, height and roofing remain estimates; an image of a hole in tiles does not create a hole in the 3D mesh.
