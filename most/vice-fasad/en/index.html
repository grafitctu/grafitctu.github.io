MOST / METHODS

Multiple façades and GIS

From two photographs to separate walls and a checked map relationship.

Identify the building, then the wall

A house number or filename is not sufficient. We compare windows, neighbours, entrances and the common corner. Two views of the same wall do not establish two façades; a portal detail is not a complete FRONT.

  1. Identity and historical period
  2. Separate photographs and FRONTs for walls A/B
  3. Geometry and UV mapping for each wall
  4. Frontal and oblique renders; text and corner checks
  5. Map relationship: position, orientation, scale and neighbours
  6. Reimported export and evidence for every claimed wall

A footprint fit is not a verified position

A model must not automatically be stretched across the whole parcel polygon. No. 191 has a broader site polygon while the model represents the front wing. Registration using a few points does not independently validate the courtyard, terrain or height.
