Mírové Square: preparation

A source catalogue for 33 structures, with 27 candidate colour FRONTs.

Preparation snapshot of 9 September 2026, based on the August backup. No new 3D model was created in this preparation. Identity, period and geometry checks are not closed for any of the 33 structures.

What is available to inspect

The local catalogue links model and photo candidates, footprints, material references and open tasks. The 33 structures describe the indexed area, not proven completeness of historical parcels. The previews below are working colour references, not a completed square scene.

Visible gaps in the evidence

The complete backup set is missing for no. 1, 2, 9, 43, 61 and 78. The GIS pilot lacks a position for 44 and 58; no. 2 and 59 have two footprint candidates. Absence from this backup is not proof that sources do not exist elsewhere.

Proposed pilot: 59 and 60, then the 55–61 row

No. 59 offers lettering and a detailed entrance, after resolving its footprint. No. 60 supports study of reveals, relief and differences between the original and cleaner FRONT. The continuous row can follow once boundaries and a common period are established.
