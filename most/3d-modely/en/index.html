Kulová: from a facade to a street

The Kulová v5 scene contains 14 buildings. In this version the new material method applies to no. 262; the surroundings are inherited from v4. Outputs include editable Blender scenes and portable GLB files, with reduced-complexity variants.

Čechova: detail and proxy volumes

Čechova v2 builds on six detailed textured houses and adds 26 GIS proxy volumes. Three house numbers without verified footprints are not placed at invented coordinates. Proxies are not counted as completed photographic reconstructions.

What a detailed render cannot guarantee

A generated surface is not a photogrammetric scan. At no. 262 a known discrepancy remains between some FRONT ornaments and geometry based on the original photograph. Rear walls, heights and hidden materials require separate assessment.
