Pavements and road surfaces

From the archival photograph to a repeatable material.

From a photograph to a surface

The black-and-white original supports the visible surface type and arrangement. Colour, dimensions, new cracks and hidden detail remain hypotheses. Colour inference, top-down reconstruction and synthesis were combined in one image-editing step.

Making a repeatable texture

Two closed periodic cuts find low-error paths through overlaps. Three-pixel local feathering joins the transitions. This does not mirror the pattern or blur the whole image. The resulting period is smaller and need not be square.

What was checked

All 27 textures have recorded visual checks of seams and corners. Repetition visibility is assessed separately. The checks do not establish physical scale, historical colour or compatibility between different variants.
