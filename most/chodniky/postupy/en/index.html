Current pavement workflows

Readable evidence → material proposal → separate variants → repeat testing.

1 · Reconstruction from a readable crop (Kostelní)

The replacement trial uses the saved 460 × 350 px crop of cp_134_001.jpg. Rows and angular setts are more legible here than in the rejected Kulová crop. The crop itself was the model input; the output is a provisional interpretation. It is a different street, not evidence for Kulová, and this trial has not passed seamless-repeat validation.

Source record · Prompt

2 · Slabs and periodic seam cuts (P004)

The foreground slabs provide readable divisions. The original P004 edit used the full photograph; its annotated crop was created later for documentation. The generated image is then processed with closed low-error cuts in X and Y and a local 3-pixel blend. Replaying those cuts reproduces the saved result; it proves processing continuity, not historical accuracy.

3 · Separate wear variants and long-strip review

B and C use the existing A proposal for material continuity together with a black-and-white source. They are separate scenarios, not measured historical deterioration. Check each repeat on its own; variants are not automatically compatible along their edges. The repeat controls preserve the original tile proportions. RGB does not replace pothole geometry.
