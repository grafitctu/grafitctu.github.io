Žatecká: the whole street in 3D from archival sources

Žatecká (renamed Československé armády in 1964) ran south-west from the centre of old Most, past the brewery, the grammar school and a row of inter-war villas. All 25 houses recorded in the MOST database were modelled from GIS footprints, archival photographs, record cards and hand-drawn site sketches. The model is untextured: no colourised references exist for this street.

Sources

  • MOST database (Libuše): 210 attachments of the 25 houses, read-only: 90 photographs, 85 record-card backs, 25 site sketches, 10 postcards (172 unique files). Every attachment was opened and is listed with its SHA-256 on the house page.
  • Archival provenance: record cards stamped by the heritage department of KSPPOP Ústí nad Labem and the District Archive in Most (XI/D). Their handwritten subject lines identify the houses, viewpoints and neighbours.
  • Site sketches give the order of houses (orientation numbers), roof shapes and plots, but no dimensions.
  • GIS layer scene-data.json (1,400 historical footprints): street axis and 14 footprints. Eleven villas (1867–1918) are missing from it.

How the villas were placed

Their order and side of the street come from the sketches and cards (orientation numbers 10–20, neighbours, the row starting after house 2073 at Na Střelišti, the rear of villa 1918 photographed from house 2072 with museum 1360 behind it). Plot widths (10–12 m), the 14.5 m building line and the start 32 m after the junction are estimates.

Method and quality control

A parametric Blender 4.5 builder turned per-house JSON plans into models; every house was rendered in ten mandatory views and audited for mesh defects. Three revisions fixed overlapping steps, concave faces, open roof wings, an eave overhang and balcony intersections. Result: 25 of 25 houses without visible geometry defects. This does not confirm historical accuracy.

Known conflicts: GIS labels of houses 1006 and 1019 are swapped relative to the archive (the model follows the archive). The brewery and the grammar school are massing models.
