Why front projection is not enough

A front-projected texture collapses into a narrow strip on perpendicular reveals. Pilaster sides, cornice undersides and return surfaces therefore need their own two-dimensional UV layout.

The mask defines where to complete

Kulová v5 uses two image-editing runs and two 4096 × 4096 atlases. Photographic context strips are pixel-protected after compositing. Completion is transferred only to designated surfaces. This is a material revision of no. 262; fourteen buildings in the scene do not mean fourteen buildings newly completed with this method.

Lettering and damage need source evidence

BEZIRKS-KRANKEN-KASSA is retained as a separate mask of the photographed lettering. The generator does not replace it with new typesetting. Materials on unseen surfaces are inferred and remain labelled provisional.
