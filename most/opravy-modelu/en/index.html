MOST / METHODS

Repairing 3D models

Removing isolated helper geometry while preserving the building.

Why a building appears too small

An oversized helper plate can enlarge the model’s bounding box. Scaling by that box makes the actual building appear too small. The repair first distinguishes an isolated helper component from architecture.

What automatic removal accepts

Only an isolated, very thin, simple component at the edge of the building core that demonstrably inflates its dimensions. Ambiguous findings are held for review. Attached roofs, cornices and plinths are not removed by this method.

How the repair was verified

Exactly one helper component with 289 vertices was removed from each example. Building geometry was compared with the original, and each GLB was reimported into Blender and rendered. Plate dimensions are not historical building measurements.

Technical cleanup does not resolve building identity, texture correctness or placement. These questions remain open for no. 259 and 260. Original models are preserved.
