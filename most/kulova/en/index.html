GRAFIT / MOST · KULOVÁ

Kulová: the whole street in 3D from archival sources

Kulová (Kugelgasse) was a short lane of about 85 m in old Most between Školní and Minoritská streets. Nine houses stood on its north side (from corner house 260 to corner house 267) and a row of 259, 258, 1608, 1712 and the single-storey 257 on the south side. All 15 houses were modelled from GIS footprints, archival photographs, record cards and building plans. The geometry is untextured; Codex adds textured houses one by one, and they appear in the viewer as the surface option “Textures (Codex)”.

What stood where

Positions come from the GIS layer, a vectorisation of the 1938 town plan. It lists Kulová as street 24 with twelve buildings; corner house 267 belongs to Minoritská, houses 259 and 154 to Školní. The database area “Kulová” holds 26 structures; twelve of them stand in neighbouring streets or far away and are not modelled. House 1712 is missing from the database, but a record card of about 1968 names “the row 258, 1608 and 1712”; the same card says that “houses under the town walls, starting with nos. 154 + 267”, were demolished around 1954, which conflicts with the GIS position of 267 at the Minoritská corner (unresolved).

Sources and geometry

All 154 attachments of the 26 structures were read (read-only) and opened: photographs, record cards, postcards, building plans from 1871 to 1940 and their colourised derivatives. A parametric Blender 4.5 builder turned per-house JSON plans into models with real window niches, dormers that open the main roof, and roof shapes checked against the hand-drawn block roof plan, sections and design sheets. House 262 was registered to the accepted colour front and checked on the original black-and-white photograph. Heights, depths and the 2 % street slope are estimates; no historical elevation data exist.
