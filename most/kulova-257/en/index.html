Original / colourized photographs

Identity and plans first

Two archive cards label house 257. An undated situation sketch supports adjacency and the complex footprint but is not a measured building survey. Heights and assignment of rear surfaces remain provisional.

Openings have real depth

G aligns geometry to registered photo boundaries. Returns have their own UVs and materials; no new glazing is invented for the demolished house. All openings were checked frontally and from both oblique sides.

The roof remains open

Photographs show an exposed timber roof and three chimneys. Joints and dimensions are estimates. The sketch shows a different roof configuration; the exact rear roof junction remains unresolved.

Materials for returns and beams

Two actual built-in image edits supplied missing material detail. Protected pixels remain unchanged after masked composition. The generated ceramic strip was rejected and replaced with a photographic roof donor.

Corrections found during review

Early renders exposed UV, brightness and roof-material problems. The comparisons retain previous states with matching cameras.

Documentation and limits

Editable Blender and embedded-texture GLB are available. Full local QA separates technical integrity from historical approval. The model remains a provisional study and is not approved for street assembly.
