FRONT references

A facade reference prepared for mapping onto a specific building face.

A visual notebook of the MOST project · September 2026

One photograph, several facades

A FRONT reference isolates the facade for use as a texture. Corner buildings require separate records for each facade. A filename or house-number link alone does not establish facade identity.

CR FRONT and GIS FRONT

A model-confirmed CR FRONT is supported by comparison with building geometry. A GIS FRONT without model confirmation remains a separate hypothesis. A generated FRONT is not a precise orthophoto: ornaments and lettering must be checked against the source photograph.

Registering the image to the model

At Kulová no. 262, registration follows individual storeys and the edges of windows, doors and cornices. Frames should map to frames and plaster to plaster. A single stretch of the whole photograph is often insufficient.
