U hřiště (1938 Seligerova, German Seligerstraße)

U hřiště (Seligerova on the 1938 town plan, German Seligerstraße, Gasgasse on older plans) was a street of old Most with three arms meeting at a bend by house 626: the south-west arm from Husovo náměstí past the SHD palace 1733 and the cinema 1313, the east-north-east arm with the rows 626–772 and 823–671 and the former gasworks (523) on its south-east side, and the arm towards náměstí Dvouletky with the row 415, 475, 416 and the cottages 439 and 417. Houses 586 to 1192 are listed under this street in GIS but face náměstí Dvouletky. We model 30 buildings in six sections; All of them are in 3D. The geometry is untextured; Codex adds textured houses one by one, and they appear in the viewer as the surface option “Textures (Codex)”.

What stood where

Positions come from the GIS layer, a vectorisation of the 1938 town plan, which lists the street as Seligerova with 36 buildings (28 with a house number, 8 without: the courtyard of the SHD palace and the former gasworks).

  • S1 · south side of the ENE arm: 626, 625, 630, 574, 772
  • S2 · north-west side of the ENE arm: 823, 831, 653, 659, 666, 673, 671
  • S3 · former gasworks: 523 a, 523 b
  • S4 · south-west arm from Husovo náměstí: 1733, 413 a, 413 b, 1313, 410
  • S5 · arm towards nám. Dvouletky: 415, 475, 416, 439, 417
  • S6 · houses facing nám. Dvouletky: 586, 1230, 1186, 1188, 1178, 1192

Houses 574 and 630: GIS buildings 634 and 635 carry swapped labels. KSPPOP sketches and captioned photographs give the order 772 | 574 | 630 | 625 | 626 from the street, so building 635 is 574 and building 634 is 630 (user decision of 25 Oct 2026; the canonical GIS and the registry are unchanged).

Structure 413: the courtyard workshops have no GIS building with this number; they are modelled on two GIS buildings without a number (1113, 1114) as 413 a and 413 b with the label “identity hypothesis”.

Not modelled: the demolished courtyard buildings 627 and 742 have no GIS footprint (blocked); structures 452, 459, 540, 552 and 740 hold only postcards or stand in other streets. Map of the street (Czech page)

Sources and geometry

Two photographic campaigns document the street: “Komplet: Starý Most” (record cards dated 28 Nov 1967, the houses still standing) and the KSPPOP documentation of the 1960s–70s (stamped cards and parcel sketches naming the neighbours), together with building plans of 1871–1940 and postcards. All 516 attachments of the 35 database structures were read live on 25 Oct 2026 (read-only, 0 writes) and opened; 149 of them are untagged low-resolution copies (1920 × 1080), which are the only facade evidence for houses 417, 1230, 1186, 1188, 1178 and 1192. Facades are read only from photographs with the house in a red frame and every opening numbered. A parametric Blender builder turns per-house JSON plans into models, which pass an automatic geometry gate (closed attic, UV, mesh, opening counts) and a visual review before they are placed in the street. Where a building project differs from the photograph (772, 410, 659, 671, 673, 1733), the model follows the photograph. Heights are estimates from plans and rectified photographs; the terrain is flat for now (provisional).
