Jan Musil: from scratches to a usable reference.

A visual notebook of the MOST project · September 2026

Start by restoring the photograph

In his FIT CTU bachelor thesis, Jan Musil investigated defect removal in historical photographs for MOST. He developed a workflow without artificial intelligence, preparing images for subsequent reconstruction.

Reading the comparison

The left image shows white scratches across the sky and facade; the right shows the cleaned result. Repairing the photograph should preserve windows and cornices. Documented damage to the building belongs to the historical evidence.
