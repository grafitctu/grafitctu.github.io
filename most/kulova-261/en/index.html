MATERIAL REVISION · V7 → V8

A darker mineral surface, a sharper facade.

The FRONT was lighter and more yellow than the colourized photograph. A masked linear-RGB correction brings the mineral surfaces closer to that reference; the window and door pixels and the plinth are protected. Plaster roughness is increased to 0.96. Geometry, UVs, cracks and existing damage are retained.

The viewer now loads the full 2048 × 2048 registered facade instead of its 1024px web copy: four times as many texels, with anisotropic filtering for oblique views. GEOMETRY REVISION · V8 → V9

A closed dormer and a stable stair base.

The old shell ended after 0.90 m, leaving an open rear and gaps between the separate front pieces. The repaired shell continues to its intersection with the sloping roof. A continuous front ring and closed recessed opening remove the see-through gaps. The hidden junction is a provisional construction, not an archival measurement.

FROM SOURCE TO MODEL

What changes during reconstruction

01 / IDENTIFY THE HOUSE

Start with the photograph

Six upper window axes, a steep roof and an upright dormer. The neighbouring roof and vegetation hide part of the ground floor. Hidden details are not direct evidence.

02 / REGISTER THE IMAGE

Every opening has its own edges

Registration uses both edges of each opening and the floor heights. The FRONT is a derived colour reference, not an exact orthophoto. Its right-hand chimney conflicts with the original; the model keeps the original left-hand position.

03 / UNWRAP THE RETURNS

The front image is not enough

Front projection collapses on perpendicular surfaces. Each surface receives two-dimensional metric UV mapping. Magenta marks areas for image completion; the remaining context is protected.

04 / COMPLETE AND CHECK

New material, protected pixels

The built-in image editor completed plaster, mineral returns, roofing and wood. Only masked pixels were composited back, with an internal boundary correction. Maximum change outside the mask: zero.

Geometry also needed correction

Ground floor. Five inherited windows were replaced by four windows and the left door. The occluded ground-floor area remains provisional.

Roof. The small upper vent visible above the main dormer was added. Fine profiles and unseen structure are approximations.

Side and rear walls. These are provisional closed surfaces; undocumented openings have not been invented.

Two iterations that failed

Duplicated bars and a broken cornice

Different polygons interpolated nonlinear coordinates differently. One pre-registered image and linear front-face UVs fixed the alignment. Glass was cropped into separate panes to prevent duplicated bars.

Door, threshold and steps
