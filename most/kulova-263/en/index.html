What the model separates

Five windows, an arched entrance and a niche have real depth. Sills and returns use separate materials. A shallow threshold and two roof openings are provisional constructions. Unobserved rear and side openings are not invented.

Corrections revealed by close views

Before/after renders preserve the same camera. Dormer rear surfaces were repaired to meet the roof. Window returns are compared with clay geometry; pipe removal is checked by hiding the independent 3D pipe. Fine synthetic facade details are not historically validated.
