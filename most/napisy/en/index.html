Historical lettering

Photographic lettering, masks and two working corner models.

Why we protect lettering during local texturing

Another reason is compatibility with Ondra Sakala’s StableGen. The project setup described here runs entirely locally, on an owned computer or a server on the local network, and uses a smaller generative network. Project experience identifies inaccurate text reproduction, especially Czech lettering and diacritics, as a weakness of this setup. We therefore transfer readable lettering from original photographs and protect it with masks against regeneration.

This limitation concerns the selected image model and configuration. StableGen supports different models; SDXL documentation, for example, explicitly identifies problems with legible text. Error rates should not be generalised to every supported model or attributed to local execution itself.

StableGen: supported models and local ComfyUI · SDXL: documented text-generation limitations

Lettering as image evidence

Readable lettering is transferred from the original through a perspective transform and a mask. We do not replace it with a modern font or guess unreadable characters. The mask bounds the transfer; verification also compares pixels outside it.
