The review can be wrong too

The hillside and distant buildings are not proven inventions: they appear in the original. For attempt two, the earlier missing-credit claim also conflicts with the retained file inspected now. The available evidence does not establish whether this was a mistaken review or a mismatch between the variant and its record.

We do not rewrite this into a seamless success story. The original decision, later correction and current observation remain separate, with unresolved uncertainty explicitly stated.

What belongs in the workflow

  1. Preserve the original and every attempt with separate hashes.
  2. Check openings, roof, architectural articulation, damage, grain, framing, caption and photographer credit, not just colour.
  3. Document the precise location and evidence for each rejection. When records conflict, reopen both the original and the exact variant file.
  4. Separate colouring visible surfaces from reconstructing hidden ones. Generated completion is not archival evidence.
  5. Record corrections to QA too. An accepted variant is not automatically historically accurate.

This example belongs to the earlier combined colourisation-and-cleanup workflow. The newer two-stage method separates these tasks. Four attempts describe this past batch, not a new allowance for extra repair attempts.
