Colourisation

From a black-and-white photograph to a colour material reference.

A visual notebook of the MOST project · September 2026

Colour as a working reference

Colourised photographs help distinguish plaster, stone, wood, metal and roofing. Material assignments should stay consistent across views of the same building. Unverified colours remain working hypotheses.

What the edit changes

The workflow preserves documented damage, openings and lettering. Foreground obstructions are removed and occluded areas completed. A colour output may therefore include reconstructed image content; it is not an original archival photograph. Plans and drawings are excluded.
