A visual notebook of the MOST project · September 2026

1,400 footprints, 132 streets

The local snapshot of 2 September 2026 contains 1,400 buildings and 132 streets. The map connects footprints with available references and models. This illustration uses the actual snapshot footprints; colours only distinguish shapes.

A footprint is not a finished reconstruction

Proxy volumes reveal urban structure before a detailed model exists for every building. Default heights of 8–17 m are deterministic working estimates, not measured historical heights. Sources from different dates must not be presented as a single historical moment.

Linking references and review

The working viewer links buildings to photographs, models and review through structure identifiers. A house-number match may be ambiguous. This public example contains only the footprint illustration; internal reviews and access data are not included.
