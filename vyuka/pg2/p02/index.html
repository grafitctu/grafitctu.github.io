Isotropic and anisotropic BRDF

  • BRDF: Bidirectional Reflectance Distribution Function.
  • Reflectance depends on illumination and viewing directions.
  • For an isotropic material, a common rotation around the normal does not change reflectance; the relative azimuth matters.
  • Anisotropic materials retain orientation-dependent highlights.

BRDF measurement setups: taxonomy

  • Gonio-reflectometers, mirror-based setups, image-based setups and portable setups.
  • Mechanical positioning may distribute four angular degrees of freedom among the sample, light and camera.

  • Capture accuracy and practical portability involve different compromises.

BSSRDF: subsurface light transport

  • Bidirectional subsurface-scattering reflectance distribution function (Nicodemus et al. 1977).
  • Scattering transports light between distinct incident and outgoing positions and directions.
  • The source describes general 9D data / reflectance fields and sparse direct measurement due to dimensionality.
  • BTF includes scattering effects, but separating them is difficult.

Fitting scattering models

  • Homogeneous dielectric materials can be described by subsurface-scattering models.
  • Jensen et al. (2001): a dipole model validated with a focused beam.
  • Separate diffuse and specular components using polarization, colour or illumination patterns; the diffuse component supports fitting scattering parameters.
  • Source references include Shafer (1985) and Nayar et al. (1997, 2006).

Specular materials: aperture diffraction

  • Light passing through a narrow aperture produces diffraction streaks.
  • Bright streaks are perpendicular to aperture blades; an odd blade count produces twice as many streaks in the illustrated situation.
  • The effect is pronounced with a small aperture and bright point light. The author proposes a circular aperture.

Dark materials: integrated quantities

  • Total hemispherical reflectance: integrate fᵣ(ωᵢ, ωᵥ) cos θᵥ over outgoing solid angle.
  • Albedo in the source: integrate ρ(ωᵢ) cos θᵢ over incident directions.
  • Total integrated scatter: TIS = Rᵈ / Rᵗ = Rᵈ / (Rˢ + Rᵈ).
  • The author’s original integral notation and geometry are preserved alongside the HTML explanation.

Vzorkování odrazivosti · GRAFIT
Syntetický 1D řez: porovnejte rovnoměrné a adaptivní vzorkování úzkého vrcholu.
Didaktický doplněk GRAFIT: normalizovaná Gaussova křivka, není fyzikální BRDF ani změřený materiál. Adaptivní metoda zde vybírá interval podle chyby lineární interpolace v jeho středu a potřebuje další zkušební vyhodnocení; stejný počet uchovaných vzorků neznamená stejný čas měření. Není to implementace metody CVPR 2013. Chyba se počítá na syntetické referenční mřížce.
