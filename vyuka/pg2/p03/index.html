Souřadnice a interpolace

Local texture space

  • N: averaged per-face normals. T: derived from geometry and texture coordinates (v,s,t).
  • B: cross product of N and T.
  • T, B and N define the transformation between world and local coordinates.

World-to-local direction transformation

  • Obtain illumination and view directions in the local texture frame.

Souřadnice a interpolace

Barycentric interpolation

  • Weights describe a point as p = w1·v1 + w2·v2 + w3·v3, with w1+w2+w3=1.
  • All three weights strictly positive: point inside the triangle.
  • A zero weight: point on the opposite edge when the other weights are nonnegative. Two zero weights: the corresponding vertex.
  • A negative weight places the point outside across the opposite edge. Source: Möbius 1827; Coxeter 1969; Fauvel et al. 1999.

Poznámka GRAFIT: interpolace vektorů obecně nezachová jejich jednotkovou délku ani kolmost. Při použití lokálního rámce je potřeba jeho normalizace a případná ortogonalizace.

Appearance rendering

  • Outgoing radiance consists of emitted and reflected radiance. The source crosses out the emitted term for its non-emissive example.
  • The rendering equation combines surface reflectance, incident radiance and the projected-area cosine over incident directions.
  • The source panel preserves the original equation and labels.

Vykreslování

Integrating measured appearance

  • Integrate reflected radiance over the hemisphere. The source notes that the foreshortening term is included in its BTF data convention.
  • A single directional-light evaluation is a limited illumination approximation.
  • Approximate the integral by a finite weighted sum of directional-light contributions.
  • The original plot compares mean pixel luminance for a leather BTF.

Anti-aliasing and mip-mapping

  • Rasterization may introduce aliasing artifacts.
  • Mip-mapping downsamples the image to lower resolutions (Gaussian pyramid).
  • Select an appropriate pyramid level for the projected texture footprint; the source describes this in terms of observer distance.

Mip-mapping and anisotropic filtering

  • The source compares mip-mapping and anisotropic filtering visually.
