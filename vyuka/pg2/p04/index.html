Half–difference parameterization

[Rusinkiewicz 1998], [Burley, Disney 2012]. Replace the incident/view pair by the half-vector and a difference direction.

The half-vector bisects incident and outgoing directions. A local frame around it describes the remaining angular difference. The original diagrams show the convention.

Half–difference coordinates: appearance

The original material renderings illustrate this parameterization.

A change of angular coordinates changes the representation of a BRDF, not the physical material.

Reprezentace

Compressing a BRDF

Splines [He et al. 1992], spherical harmonics [Westin et al. 1992], spherical wavelets [Schröder & Sweldens 1995], and Zernike polynomials [Koenderink et al. 1996].

A compact basis trades storage and evaluation cost against approximation error. Smooth bases can blur narrow highlights.

Reprezentace

Factorizing measured reflectance

[Kautz et al. 1999]: SVD decomposition into products of lower-dimensional functions.

[McCool et al. 2001]: homomorphic factorization with positive factors. [Suykens et al. 2003]: chained matrix factorization.

The lecture describes measured-data approximations evaluated through textures for interactive rendering.

Fotometrické stereo

Photometric stereo

A single intensity observation cannot distinguish surface orientation from reflectance.

Keep the camera fixed and vary known illumination. Recover surface normals and albedo; integrate normals separately if a height map is required.

This differs from geometric stereo, which changes viewpoint.

Jiří Filip · Původní PDF · strana 6

Fotometrické stereo

The Lambertian observation model

For calibrated directional lights, scaled normal g = ρn converts the Lambertian model into a linear system.

Assumptions: linear camera response, known lighting, locally Lambertian reflectance, no cast shadows, saturation or interreflection in the selected observations.

Doplněk GRAFIT: alespoň tři lineárně nezávislé směry světel. Úhlové optimum je nutné vztáhnout k jasně definované normále či rovině.

Ik=ρn·lk
g=ρn

Jiří Filip · Původní PDF · strana 7

Fotometrické stereo

Calibrated photometric stereo

Stack light directions into matrix L and observed intensities into I. With three independent lights, solve for g.

The length of g yields albedo in the selected illumination convention; normalize g to obtain the surface normal.

Zero albedo gives no normal information. Near-singular lighting makes the estimate unstable.

I=Lg
g=L−1I
ρ=‖g‖,n=g/ρ

Fotometrické stereo

More than three lights

An overdetermined system uses least squares. More observations can improve robustness to random noise when the model is appropriate.

Doplněk GRAFIT: použijte QR nebo SVD místo explicitní inverze normálních rovnic. Více světel samo neodstraní systematickou chybu modelu.

g^=L+I
L+=(LTL)−1LT(rank(L)=3)

Fotometrické stereo

Robust photometric stereo

Specularities and shadows violate the simple Lambertian model.

Use robust estimation or reject inconsistent observations instead of letting a few outliers dominate the fit.

Fotometrické stereo

Photometric stereo: extensions

The source compares three-light estimation with redundant least squares.

Extensions include weighted least squares, RANSAC, colour observations, near-field lights, non-Lambertian models, uncalibrated lighting and learned methods. These change the assumptions and unknowns.

Analytické modely

Analytical BRDF models

Incident direction i and outgoing direction v are parameterized by polar and azimuthal angles.

Many analytical models combine diffuse and specular terms. A Gaussian lobe is one possible approximation of a highlight.

fr(i,v)=kd/π+ksL(i,v)

Reflection geometry

The original diagram contrasts diffuse reflection, specular reflection and microgeometry effects.

Masking and shadowing reduce the set of microfacets visible to both light and camera.

Analytické modely

Phong and Blinn–Phong

Phong combines ambient, diffuse and a power of the reflected-light/view alignment. Blinn–Phong uses the normal/half-vector alignment.

Doplněk GRAFIT: normalizujte směry a omezte záporné skalární součiny. Historický Phongův osvětlovací vzorec bez normalizace nelze automaticky vydávat za energeticky konzervativní BRDF.

r=2(i·n)n−i
h=(i+v)/‖i+v‖

Jiří Filip · Původní PDF · strana 14

Analytické modely

Schlick, Lafortune and Ashikhmin–Shirley

Schlick approximates the Fresnel term using reflectance at normal incidence.

Lafortune generalizes cosine lobes. Ashikhmin–Shirley includes anisotropic highlights and an energy-aware diffuse/specular combination.

Roughness shapes the distribution of microfacet normals; it is a different quantity from Fresnel reflectance.

F=F0+(1−F0)(1−cosθ)5

Jiří Filip · Původní PDF · strana 15

Analytické modely

Microfacet models

Torrance–Sparrow [1967] and Cook–Torrance [1981]: distribute small facets and combine Fresnel F, normal distribution D and geometry G.

He et al. [1991] account for wave-optical effects. The isotropy of a particular source model does not mean that every microfacet model is isotropic.

Analytické modely

Ward and anisotropic reflectance

Ward [1992] uses an exponential lobe and physically meaningful parameters; Duer [2005] discusses normalization.

The anisotropic variant controls widths along two local axes. Its normalization is model-specific; do not mix factors from different BRDF conventions.

fr=kdπ+ks1cosθicosθve−tan2θh/α24πα2
LWard=F(F0,v·h)παxαyQe−S
Q=14(i·h)2hz4
S=hx2/αx2+hy2/αy2hz2

Analytické modely

Oren–Nayar and Schlick models

Oren–Nayar describes rough diffuse reflection using Lambertian facets.

Schlick presents layered/variable-complexity approximations, including effects beyond a single smooth surface.

Explicit microgeometry and threads

Westin and colleagues model microgeometry explicitly; fitting and integration can be demanding.

Thread-based BSDFs and woven-fabric models include the structure of yarns: [Sadeghi et al. 2013], [Xia et al. 2025].

Analytické modely

GGX: a complete specular model

h is the normalized half-vector. αx and αy are strictly positive roughness parameters along local tangent axes.

The expressions below use a consistent α notation for the source’s mixed α/σ symbols (redakční sjednocení GRAFIT). Both directions must lie above the local surface.

The separable Smith geometry term is the form shown in this lecture. Other correlated variants exist and must not be silently substituted.

fGGX=F(F0,v·h)D(h)G(i,v)4cosθicosθv
D(h)=1παxαy(hx2/αx2+hy2/αy2+hz2)2
G=1(1+Λ(i))(1+Λ(v))
Λ(v)=−1+1+(αx2vx2+αy2vy2)/vz22

Analytické modely

Disney principled reflectance

[Burley 2012]: an artist-oriented model combining diffuse reflection, a GGX/GTR2 specular lobe, GTR1 clearcoat and sheen.

Controls include base colour, metallic, roughness, specular level, anisotropy, sheen and clearcoat. They are coordinated model parameters, not independent measured physical laws.

Analytické modely

Fitting analytical models

Choose initial parameters, then optimize roughness, Fresnel response and anisotropy orientation. Some coefficients can be solved linearly while shape parameters require nonlinear fitting.

Loss function, sampling, initialization and local minima matter. The source mentions ALTA and MATLAB; these are source tools, not current-version recommendations.

Analytické modely

Layered BSDFs

Interfaces, scattering and absorption can be combined through multiple transport between layers.

A layered BSDF is not generally a simple sum of independent lobes. Plane-parallel approximations omit lateral transport that requires a spatial scattering model.

Kalibrované fotometrické stereo · GRAFIT
Výpočetní doplněk GRAFIT: syntetické intenzity, nikoli naměřená data. Tři jednotková světla mají azimut 0°,120°,240° a polární úhel 35° od z. Normála se odklání nejvýše o35°, takže bez přidané odchylky nejsou stíny. I=ρ(n·l); konstanty osvětlení včetně Lambertova faktoru jsou absorbovány do kalibrace. Odchylka se přičítá jen první intenzitě, záporný výsledek se ořízne na0. Přesně tři rovnice nemají redundanci pro detekci chyby. Zelená: skutečná normála; oranžová: odhad; graf zobrazuje pouze projekci xz.
