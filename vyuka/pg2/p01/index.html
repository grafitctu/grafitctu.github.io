Appearance rendering

Total outgoing radiance consists of emitted and reflected radiance:

Lo(x, ωo) = Le(x, ωo) + Lr(x, ωo)

Lr(x, ωo) = ∫Ω⁺ fr(x, ωi, ωo) Li(x, ωi) (n · ωi) dωi

Surface reflectance × incident illumination × projected area, integrated over incoming directions.

Radiometry

  • Irradiance E = dΦ / dA, in W m⁻².
  • Radiance L = d²Φ / (dA cos θ dω), in W m⁻² sr⁻¹.
  • BRDF fᵣ = dLₒ / dEᵢ, in sr⁻¹.
  • Directional albedo integrates reflected energy over outgoing directions.
  • Mean albedo averages directional albedo over incident directions.

GRAFIT · pomůcka

Irradiance popisuje osvětlení plochy; radiance navíc rozlišuje směr. Jednotky jsou vysvětlující doplněk, úplné autorské vzorce jsou zachované v ilustraci.

Jiří Filip · datum podkladu neuvedeno · PDF, strana 14

Radiometry: physical constraints

  • Helmholtz reciprocity: fᵣ(ωᵢ, ωₒ) = fᵣ(ωₒ, ωᵢ).
  • Energy conservation: ∫Ω⁺ fᵣ(ωᵢ, ωₒ) cos θₒ dωₒ ≤ 1.
  • Isotropy: invariance to simultaneous rotation of the incoming and outgoing directions around the normal.

Bidirectional reflectance distribution function

fr(ωi, ωo) = dLo(ωo) / dEi(ωi)

  • Source/sensor direction reciprocity.
  • Energy conservation: reflected fraction between 0 and 1.
  • Non-negativity.

Bidirectional texture function

  • BTF is a texture dependent on illumination and viewing directions.
  • Captures inter-reflections, subsurface scattering, local masking and shadowing.
  • Thousands of images per material; data may occupy gigabytes.
  • Compression and modelling are necessary.

Spatially varying BRDFs

  • Restrictions: opaque surfaces, no subsurface scattering.
  • Nearly flat surfaces: no occlusion/masking and no interaction between pixels.
  • Simpler local transport allows spatial clustering and sparse measurements.
  • Examples: lacquered wood and wallpaper.
