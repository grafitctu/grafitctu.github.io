Visit by students from Kanazawa University

Students from Japan visited SAGELab and ggLab, learned what we do at FIT and tried the touch wall and student games.

From Kanazawa to Prague

The visit connected international student exchange with a tour of our laboratories, projects and interactive installations. At SAGELab and ggLab, the visitors explored our facilities, learned about our work at FIT and tried the demonstrations themselves.

Escape from Brno was the clear favourite among the games and installations — even for visitors who had travelled all the way from Japan.
